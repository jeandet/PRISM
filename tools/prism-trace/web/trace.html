PRISM trace

Reads a profile dump from a board over USB and opens it in Perfetto. Nothing is uploaded: the trace goes from this page straight to the Perfetto tab.

  1. Connect and pick the board's serial port. Its console appears below.
  2. Press the board's dump button (MODE on the ESP32-S31-Korvo-1, the blue user button on the STM32H7B3I-DK).
  3. Open in Perfetto when the dump has arrived.
